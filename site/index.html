Model quality

Anomalies in the published series

Thresholds were fixed on 2019-2022 before any later year was examined. The detectors look for gaps, values above capacity or below zero, frozen values, shifted timestamps, clock-change errors, breaks in level, outliers against the model, and series that disagree with their parts or with another source.

What the published series contain

The Dutch series misses a third of Eurostat's wind output and almost all its solar output, so its level points to the distribution grids being left out. No statement of TenneT, its producer, is archived to confirm it, and the evaluation plan publishes no other country until this validation case passes.

Output held back at negative prices

The potential minus the published output at hours with a negative day-ahead price, with a 90 % bootstrap interval. France is set against the volumes RTE publishes. Germany is set against Hirth (2026), whose wind includes offshore farms. Denmark has no published reference: its years are shown only above the minimal detectable effect.
