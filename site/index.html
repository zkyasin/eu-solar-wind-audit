Solar and onshore wind · France and Germany · 2019–2025

1.58TWh of solar output held back in mainland France in 2025, at negative prices

Published series cannot show what a fleet did not inject. This study rebuilds, hour by hour, the output each French and German solar and wind fleet could have produced from ERA5 weather and its installed capacity, then uses it as an independent reference to audit what grid operators publish.

01 Modelled output

A reference that never sees what it checks

ERA5 hourly weather on its 0.25° grid drives pvlib on the solar capacity of each French département and German Kreis, and turbine power curves at hub height for onshore wind. The capacity comes from the SDES and the Marktstammdatenregister.

A few parameters are calibrated on hours priced 5 €/MWh or more, leaving each year out in turn, then frozen. The potential of a year is therefore fitted on the other years, and never on the hours it is used to judge.

02 Anomalies

Where the published series go wrong

Every hourly series of éCO2mix (France and its 12 regions) and SMARD (Germany and its four control areas, with Energy-Charts as a second source) goes through rule-based detectors, and statistical detectors on its residual against the model.

Thresholds were fixed on 2019–2022 before any later year was read. A finding is not a defect: each one is either checked against the raw files and written as a ticket, or set aside with its reason.

All findings and the tickets

Four tickets, checked against the raw files

FR-1 éCO2mix, all 26 solar and wind series

1 empty half-hour

The row that closes 31 December 2025 has no solar, wind or consumption value, nationally and in each of the 12 regions. Every earlier year closes complete.

FR-4 Nouvelle-Aquitaine wind, January 2023

231 GWh for about 414 expected

A drop on 2 January and its reversal on 31 January, while the neighbouring regions do not move. The national total, the sum of the regions, carries the deficit.

DE-1 TransnetBW solar, December 2025

207 hours at zero

Published as 0.0 while the German total keeps the output of the area: up to 15.5 GWh a day above the sum of its four control areas.

DE-4 TransnetBW solar, 12 April 2019

about 3 GWh missing

Zero from 16:00 to 20:00 in the area and in the national total, which still equals the sum of its parts. Only the model shows the hole.

Time conventions are checked too: éCO2mix publishes the same rows for every local day, as documented, which repeats the hour after each spring clock change and leaves out the hour before each autumn one. The daily figures of this page are built on local days, 23 or 25 hours long at the changes.
